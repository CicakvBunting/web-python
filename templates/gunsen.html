Quick Turn Structure & Reminders

Turn Sequence

  1. Choose Order Card: Take one face-up card from the row (paying resource cost if applicable).
  2. Place Order Card: Place it in a matching column on your board OR place face-down for a Noble Action.
  3. Activate Column / Noble Action: Activate all cards in that column top-to-bottom, OR pay Influence for a Noble Action.
  4. Resolve Battle: If movement entered an opponent territory, resolve combat.
  5. End Turn: Refill the order row to 5 cards.

Frequently Overlooked Rules

  • Heroes: Must always stay with an army or at your fort. They do not count toward the 5-troop limit and cannot be permanently lost.
  • Column Compounding: Every time you add a card to a column, you activate every card above it. Building tall creates massive mid-game swing turns!
  • Row Clearing: Before drafting, you may optionally clear all 5 cards if 4 or more share the same color or resource cost.
